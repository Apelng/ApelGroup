About the Offer

The Dangote Petroleum Refinery and Petrochemicals FZE Initial Public Offering comprises 4.1 billion ordinary shares offered at ₦525 per share.

The offer opened on 14 September 2026 and is scheduled to close on 13 October 2026, subject to the terms contained in the offer documents.

The offer is open to retail, institutional and eligible African investors, subject to applicable eligibility requirements.

How to Subscribe Through Apel

Submit your subscription through Apel Asset Limited using the official offer portal.

  1. Visit the Apel offer portal. Go to offers.apel.com.ng and select the Dangote Refinery IPO.
  2. Enter your details. Provide your BVN and the other information requested on the subscription form.
  3. Enter your number of shares. The minimum subscription is 10 Offer Shares, with subsequent subscriptions in multiples of 10.
  4. Review and submit. Review your application and complete payment through the official payment channel shown on the portal.
